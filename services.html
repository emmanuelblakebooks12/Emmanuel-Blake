COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do we communicate during the project?

Everything is handled via email. I document all strategies, updates, and deliverables in writing so you have a clear, organized reference — no calls, no meetings, ever.

How long does a typical campaign take?

Most campaigns run 4–12 weeks depending on scope, though ongoing marketing support is available for authors looking for long-term growth.

Do you work with self-published and traditionally published authors?

Yes — I work with self-published authors, hybrid authors, traditionally published authors, and small to mid-size publishers.

What genres do you specialize in?

I've worked across fiction (thriller, romance, fantasy, literary) and nonfiction (self-help, business, memoir). Email me your genre, and I'll let you know how my experience applies.

Can I combine multiple services instead of choosing a package?

Absolutely. Packages are a starting guide — every campaign is ultimately customized. Tell me what you need, and I'll build around it.

How do I get started?

Simply email me your book details and goals, and I'll respond within 24–48 hours with a tailored strategy proposal.
