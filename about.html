WHO I AM

Hi, I'm Emmanuel Blake — Your Book Marketing Specialist

I help authors, self-publishers, and small publishing houses turn their books into titles that actually get discovered, read, and talked about. My work sits at the intersection of marketing strategy and storytelling — because I believe a book's marketing should feel like a natural extension of the story itself, not a disconnected sales pitch.

Over the years, I've worked across nearly every genre — thrillers, romance, fantasy, self-help, business, memoir, and more — and I've learned that the biggest reason good books fail to sell isn't bad writing. It's invisibility. Readers can't buy a book they never see. My job is to close that gap: to put your book directly in front of the people most likely to love it, using the right mix of platforms, timing, and messaging.

My process is built entirely around email communication. Every strategy document, every campaign update, every performance report is written and sent to you directly — creating a clear, organized paper trail you can reference at any point in our work together. No rushed phone calls, no forgotten meeting notes — just clean, professional, documented communication from start to finish.

MY JOURNEY

My Journey in Book Marketing

From freelance beginnings to becoming a dedicated book marketing specialist.

2017

Started in Digital Marketing

Began freelancing in general digital marketing, working with small businesses on social media and email campaigns — building the foundational skills that would later shape my book marketing approach.

2019

Found My Niche in Book Marketing

After helping a close friend's self-published thriller reach Amazon's bestseller list using a custom-built launch strategy, I realized book marketing was where I wanted to focus my entire career.

2021

Expanded Into Full-Service Campaigns

Grew from single-service offerings into complete, end-to-end launch campaigns — combining Amazon ads, PR outreach, email marketing, and author branding into one cohesive strategy.

2022

Added Book Club & Community Outreach

Recognized the power of book clubs and reading communities in driving sustained sales, and built dedicated outreach systems to tap into this often-overlooked channel.

2023

Reached 150+ Authors Served

Hit a major milestone working with authors and small publishers across fiction, nonfiction, and niche genres around the world.

TODAY

Helping Authors Like You

Continuing to test, measure, and refine data-driven strategies — always communicated through a clean, documented, email-based process that keeps every client informed and in control.
