Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

A few quick answers to questions I get asked most often about getting started.

Do you offer phone calls or virtual meetings?

No. My entire business runs on email. I've found that a written process keeps communication clear, organized, and easy to reference at any point in our work together — no scheduling conflicts, no time zone issues, just clear, documented communication.

How soon will I hear back after reaching out?

I respond to all inquiries within 24–48 hours, Monday through Saturday. If you don't see a reply in your inbox after two business days, please check your spam folder before following up.

What should I include in my first email?

Your book's title and genre, a brief description, your current marketing goals, and any specific services you're interested in. The more context you provide, the more tailored and useful my first response will be.

Can I request a specific service instead of a full package?

Absolutely. While many authors choose one of the three main packages, all 18 services can be booked individually or combined into a custom plan built around your specific goals and budget.
